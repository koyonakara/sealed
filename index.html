Why

A reason that fits Dusk, not a gamble.

Collectibles feel speculative. These aren't. They're participation proofs in the style of a pressed seal reflecting Dusk's own values of verifiable settlement, clean accounting, and no front-running. You earn an edition by doing something; the chain remembers it.
